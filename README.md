## ♟️ Chess & Code: Tactical Architecture

As an internationally rated Indian National Chess Player with **100+ tournament victories**, I find that the deep calculation, pattern recognition, and strategic foresight required on the board map directly to software design. 

Here is how I view the board through the eyes of an engineer:

*   👑 **The King (Core Logic):** The vital heart of the system. Needs absolute security through robust authentication (**JWT**, **Keycloak**, **MFA**) and secure encryption.
*   ♛ **The Queen (Spring Boot):** The most powerful piece on the board. Moves in all directions to coordinate backend frameworks, REST APIs, and microservices.
*   ♜ **The Rooks (Databases & Storage):** The heavy, solid pillars of persistence. Supporting the system with highly scalable distributed databases (**TiDB**, **MySQL**, **MongoDB**) and low-latency cache (**Redis**).
*   ♞ **The Knights (Event Streams):** Asynchronous and tactical. Jumping over hurdles via **RabbitMQ** to decouple microservices and process data out-of-band.
*   ♝ **The Bishops (SOLID & Design Patterns):** Moving diagonally with precision. Guiding clean code architecture (SRP, OCP, LSP, ISP, DIP) and maintainable structural patterns.
*   ♟️ **The Pawns (Concurrency & Threads):** Small, parallel worker units (**Multithreading**, **Thread Pools**) marching forward to process concurrent tasks efficiently.
